What We Do

Everything included. Nothing hidden.

  • Full tear-off of existing shingles and underlayment

  • GAF Timberline® shingles, 30-year warranty
  • New drip edge, ridge cap, and flashing

Pricing & Expectations

From $4.50/sq ft

Most residential replacements run $8,500–$18,000 depending on home size, pitch, and material grade. We provide written, itemized estimates with no hidden fees. Financing available from $189/month.

How It Works

Your replacement, step by step

01

Free Drone Assessment

We fly a drone to map your roof's condition, identify soft spots, and measure square footage. No ladders, no guessing.

02

Material Selection

Choose from 3 shingle grades, 8 color palettes, and optional upgraded underlayment. We provide physical samples.

03

Full Tear-Off & Install

Our crew arrives at 7 AM, completes tear-off, installs ice barrier, new decking (if needed), and all new shingles, typically in one day.

04

Final Walkthrough & Warranty

We walk the entire roof with you, point out every detail, and register your manufacturer warranty on the spot.
